A DIFFERENT KIND OF ISLAND

Sri Lanka changes
as you travel.

Leave the coast and the air gets cooler. Head inland and ancient cities appear among forests and reservoirs. Keep going and tea-covered mountains rise into the mist. Then return to the ocean for a completely different rhythm.

01 / THE COAST

Where the
island meets the sea.

From relaxed southern bays to the surf of the east, Sri Lanka's coastline is not one beach experience. It changes with the coast, the season and the mood of your trip.
