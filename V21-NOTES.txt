V21 — Real Booking Engine + Payment Architecture

Customer side:
- Verified supplier cards now expose Request booking.
- Booking modal captures customer, arrival date, travellers, service and message.
- Public POST /api/bookings creates a booking request only for active Verified suppliers.
- Customer receives a booking ID and clear no-payment-yet state.

Booking lifecycle:
New → Reviewing → Confirmed → payment-ready → Completed / Declined

Payment architecture:
- booking_requests now stores quoted_amount, currency, payment_status and payment_reference.
- POST /api/bookings/:id/payment-intent creates a provider-neutral payment reference only after supplier confirmation + quote.
- No card details are accepted or stored by Lankaora.
- Connect a PCI-compliant payment provider checkout at the marked integration point.

Admin:
- GET /api/bookings/admin
- PATCH /api/bookings/admin/:id for status + quote management.

Important:
- Real payments are intentionally not activated without provider credentials/configuration.
- Prices and availability are not invented; suppliers confirm final quote and availability.
