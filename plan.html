WHEN TO VISIT

Different coasts.
Different seasons.

Sri Lanka is influenced by two monsoons and two inter-monsoon periods. That means the best coast or region can change with the month.

DEC–FEBSouth & West Coast
MAY–SEPEast Coast & dry zone
MAR–APRTransition season
OCT–NOVIsland-wide showers

RESPONSIBLE TRAVEL

Leave the island
better than you found it.

Respect wildlife, follow site rules, dress appropriately at religious places, reduce waste and choose local businesses where possible. Responsible travel is part of experiencing Sri Lanka well.
